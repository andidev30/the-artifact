Personal data of your members' own accounts (their email, name and sign-in methods) is covered by our [Privacy Policy](/legal/privacy), where we are the controller.

## What we do

1. **We follow your instructions.** We process the data only to run the service for you, as the Terms and this addendum describe, and as you set it up in the app. If the law requires other processing, we tell you first unless the law forbids it.
2. **We keep it confidential.** Only we have access to the systems that hold your data, and only when needed to run the service.
3. **We keep it secure.** HTTPS for every connection. Tokens, passwords and recovery codes are stored as hashes; authenticator secrets are encrypted. Pages run in a sandbox that keeps them away from the app and your members' cookies. Access checks on every request. Rate limits on sign-in and other sensitive actions. Backups by our database provider.
4. **We use sub-processors carefully.** You allow us to use the providers on our [Sub-processors](/legal/subprocessors) page. Each is bound by data protection terms at least as protective as this addendum. We update that page before we add or replace one. If you object for a reasonable data protection reason, tell us within 14 days. If we can't solve it, you can stop using the service and delete your organization.
5. **We help with requests from people.** When someone asks to see, correct or delete their data, most of it can be done in the app. If you need more, email us and we will help. If a request about your organization's data comes to us, we pass it to you.
6. **We tell you about breaches.** If a breach affects your organization's personal data, we tell your owners by email without undue delay, and in time for you to give the 3 × 24 hour notice that UU PDP requires. We tell you what happened, what data is affected, and what we are doing about it.
7. **We delete the data at the end.** When you delete a page or your organization, the data is deleted from the service at once, and its files within a day. Copies in backups expire within 30 days. Before you delete, you can download every page as a zip file. To delete an organization that still has other members, email us from an owner's address.
8. **We answer your questions.** We answer reasonable written questions about how we protect your data, so you can check that we meet this addendum.

If the GDPR applies to your organization's data, this addendum is meant to cover what Article 28 of the GDPR asks of a processor. If you need the EU Standard Contractual Clauses, email us.
