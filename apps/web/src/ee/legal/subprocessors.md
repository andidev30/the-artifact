# Sub-processors

Last updated: 28 September 2026

These are the service providers that handle personal data for The Artifact's hosted service at {{APP_URL}}. Each one only gets the data it needs for its job, under its own data processing terms.

## Vercel Inc.

- **Company based in:** United States
- **What it does for us:** runs the website and our servers (in Tokyo, Japan), runs scheduled jobs, keeps request logs, and provides Web Analytics and Speed Insights
- **Data it handles:** everything the service stores passes through it, request logs, and website analytics without cookies

## Supabase Inc.

- **Company based in:** United States
- **What it does for us:** runs our database and file storage, and keeps their backups
- **Data it handles:** accounts, organizations, sharing, comments, and every page's files

## Our email delivery provider

- **What it does for us:** sends sign-in links, invitations, share and comment notices over SMTP, and forwards contact sales messages to us
- **Data it handles:** email addresses, names, page titles and links, comment text, and contact sales messages

## Payments

We don't take payments yet. When we do, we will add the payment provider here first.

## Other services you may choose

- **Google.** If you sign in with Google, Google tells us your email, name and profile picture link. Google acts on its own account here, under its own privacy policy, not as our sub-processor.
- **Files a page loads.** A page can load scripts, fonts or images from other sites. Those sites see the visitor's request. The page's author chooses them, not us.

## Changes

When we add or replace a sub-processor, we update this page and its date first. Organizations covered by our [Data Processing Addendum](/legal/dpa) can ask to be told by email, and can object as the addendum describes.
